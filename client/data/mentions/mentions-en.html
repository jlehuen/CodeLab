CodeLab IDE and Simulators

An educational tool to learn coding
with much more than screen-keyboard interactions.

CodeLab is an educational environment dedicated to learning programming. Its originality is that it offers playful or technical alternatives to traditional screen-keyboard interactions for the design of educational activities, through the use of application modules. For more information, visit the CodeLab website. In particular, you can contribute to the development of application plugins. Tutorials and development kits are available on this site.

To contact us, click on this link and let us know your expectations and comments.
